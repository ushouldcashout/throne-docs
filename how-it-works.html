how the desk works

the venue in one page: who trades, who funds, who gets paid.

the desk

The desk is the trading venue at trade.throne.network. It lists perpetual futures on stocks and crypto, settles in USD terms, and runs on Robinhood Chain. Orders match on a central limit order book with a visible depth, last trades, and a chart. Every trade pays a fee to the desk.

the vault

The vault is the capital behind the venue. People who fund it are the reason the desk can run deep books and pay out programs. The fees the desk earns flow to them. That is the whole model: traders pay fees, the fees pay the court.

the court

The court is the name for the people who fund the vault and hold its seats. Priority access to the court is the reward for the most active traders each season. We publish who qualifies and when on @thronedefi; the mechanics of the court itself are announced there, not here.
