getting help

Post or DM @thronedefi with your wallet address (never your seed phrase) and what happened, with a screenshot if you have one. For a stuck deposit or withdrawal include the chain and the transaction hash.

how to know it's us

  • We announce from @thronedefi only.
  • We never DM first.
  • We never ask for keys, seed phrases, or for you to sign anything outside the desk.
  • Our only trading URL is trade.throne.network. Type it.

reporting a bug

Describe what you did, what you expected, what happened, your browser and wallet, and the time in UTC. If the desk showed an error, paste it. We read everything.

status

If the desk is degraded we say so on @thronedefi first. If you cannot reach the desk and there is no post, check your connection and try a hard refresh before assuming the worst.
