the court and $THRONE.

what the fees are for.

the short version

The desk earns fees. The fees pay the people who fund the vault. Those people are the court.

Traders never need to think about this; the desk works the same whether you care about the court or not. It matters if you want to be on the other side of the trade: funding the venue instead of only using it.

priority access

The way in for traders is the season leaderboard. Top ranks each season get priority access to the court. What that access looks like for season zero, and how many get it, is announced on @thronedefi when the competition is announced.

$THRONE.

$THRONE. is the token of the venue. Details on its role, supply and any distribution are announced on @thronedefi and written here when they are public. Anything you read elsewhere before that is not from us.
