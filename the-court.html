the court and $THRONE.

what the fees are for.

the short version

The desk earns fees. The fees pay the people who fund the vault. Those people are the court.

Traders never need to think about this; the desk works the same whether you care about the court or not. It matters if you want to be on the other side of the trade: funding the venue instead of only using it.

how the vaults work

Two pages explain the court as a depositor sees it, step by step: the senior vault (deposit USDG, hold a weekly-priced share) and the junior vault (stake $THRONE., stand first in line for losses, take the court's income).

$THRONE.

$THRONE. is the token of the venue. Further details on its role and supply are announced on @thronedefi and written here when they are public. Anything you read elsewhere before that is not from us.
