the court and $THRONE.

what the fees are for.

the short version

The desk earns fees. The fees pay the people who fund the vault. Those people are the court.

Traders never need to think about this; the desk works the same whether you care about the court or not. It matters if you want to be on the other side of the trade: funding the venue instead of only using it.

priority access

The way in for traders is the board. The sixteen pieces on each side, ranked by season points, get priority access to the court. How the pieces are scored is on the points and King Drop page. What that access looks like for season zero is announced on @thronedefi.

$THRONE.

$THRONE. is the token of the venue. Holding it through a week earns more points on the desk (100,000 for 1.25x, 500,000 for 1.5x), and 3% of supply is distributed to traders through the weekly King Drop. Further details on its role and supply are announced on @thronedefi and written here when they are public. Anything you read elsewhere before that is not from us.
