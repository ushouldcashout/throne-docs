start here

throne. is a desk for stocks and crypto perps, open 24/7, on robinhood chain. the fees it earns pay the people who fund the vault.

what you can do

  • Trade perpetual futures on major stocks and crypto, around the clock, including when the stock market is closed.
  • Fund with USDG on Robinhood Chain, or send USDC from a supported chain and it lands in the same account.
  • Pay one flat fee: 4.5 bps taker, 0 bps maker. No tiers, no hidden lines.
  • Earn points on every taker trade during season zero, and 40% of the desk's fee revenue on trades made by people you refer.

three things to know before your first trade

  1. Leverage is a loan against your margin. The desk shows a risk rate on every position. At 100% the position is liquidated. The desk plays a siren at 80% so you hear it before you see it.
  2. Stock perps trade when the stock market doesn't. Outside regular hours the perp has its own price, anchored by funding. See markets and hours.
  3. Referral codes bind once, at sign-up. If someone referred you, enter their code before your first trade. It cannot be changed later.
