deposits and withdrawals

fund in one step, from where your money already is.

what the desk accepts

chain asset
Robinhood Chain USDG
Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Avalanche, Mantle, Sei, Sonic, Berachain, Abstract, X Layer, and others listed in the deposit dialog USDC

Whatever you send, your account balance shows in USD terms and every market on the desk is available. Robinhood Chain is the desk's home and appears first in the list; the other chains are there so you do not have to bridge before you trade.

depositing

  1. Connect your wallet and sign in (one signature, no gas).
  2. Open Deposit from the account menu or the order form.
  3. Pick the chain your funds are on, enter the amount, approve if asked, confirm.
  4. Deposits from Robinhood Chain credit within a minute. Deposits from other chains take a few minutes while they cross.

The desk shows an estimated arrival time in the dialog. The minimum deposit is shown there too.

withdrawing

  1. Open Withdraw from the account menu.
  2. Pick the chain you want to receive on and the amount.
  3. Confirm. Funds arrive as USDG on Robinhood Chain or USDC on the other chains.

Withdrawals need free collateral: you cannot withdraw margin that is holding a position open. If a withdrawal is partly blocked, the dialog tells you how much is available and why.

fees on moving money

The desk does not charge to deposit or withdraw. You pay the network's gas on the sending chain, and cross-chain withdrawals include the bridge's cost, shown before you confirm.

subaccounts

You can create subaccounts from the account menu to separate strategies. Each has its own balance, positions and risk rate. Transfers between your accounts are instant and free.
