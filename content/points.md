# points and season zero

every taker trade earns points. season zero runs 25 september to 2 december 2026.

## how points accrue

- **1 point per $10 of taker volume.** A $10,000 market order earns 1,000 points.
- Maker fills (resting limit orders) earn **no points**. Points reward traders who take liquidity and pay the fee.
- Points are counted per account, in real time, and shown at [throne.network/points](https://throne.network/points) and in the desk's **Points** tab.
- Points never expire within a season. Each season starts from zero.

## what points are for

Points decide your rank on the season leaderboard. Rank decides who gets **priority access to the court** and who shares the season's prizes. The specific ladder and prizes for season zero are published on @thronedefi and on the points page when the competition is announced, and mirrored here the same day.

## anti-wash rules

Points exist to reward real taking. To keep it that way:

- Only taker fills count.
- Per-account caps apply to volume that round-trips within seconds.
- Obvious wash patterns (matching your own orders across accounts, back-and-forth with a partner at no risk) are reviewed manually and zeroed.
- Rankings are final only at season end, after review.

## what points are not

Points are not a token, not a claim on anything, and carry no promise of future value. They are a leaderboard.
