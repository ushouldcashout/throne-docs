# account and security

your keys, your account, and what the desk never asks for.

## connecting

The desk works with EVM wallets (browser extensions and mobile wallets via WalletConnect). Connecting does not move funds. Signing in creates a trading key for this device so you can place orders without signing every one; you can revoke it any time from **Portfolio → API keys**.

## what the desk will never do

- Ask for your seed phrase or private key, anywhere, ever.
- DM you first on X, Telegram or Discord. Anyone doing that in our name is not us.
- Send you a link to "verify", "migrate" or "claim". Programs are announced on @thronedefi and paid to your desk balance automatically.

Bookmark [trade.throne.network](https://trade.throne.network) and type it yourself. Check the URL before you sign.

## sign-in and sessions

Your trading key lives in your browser. Clearing site data or switching browsers means signing in again. Each sign-in is one wallet signature and costs nothing.

## restricted regions

Some regions cannot use the desk. If yours is one of them, the desk tells you when you connect. Using a VPN to get around that is a breach of the [terms](https://throne.network/terms).

## referral codes at sign-up

If someone referred you, enter their code when you first sign in or on the Referrals page before your first trade. Codes bind permanently and cannot be changed or removed later.
