# referrals

bring traders to the desk, earn a share of what they pay, every day.

## how it works

1. Trade **$6,969** of volume on the desk. That unlocks **Create Code** on the [Referrals page](https://trade.throne.network/referrals).
2. Pick a code (letters and numbers, 4 to 15 characters). Your link is `trade.throne.network/?ref=YOURCODE`.
3. Anyone who signs up with your code or link is your referral, permanently.
4. You earn **40% of the desk's fee revenue** on every trade they make, for as long as they trade. Paid daily at **00:00 UTC** to your desk balance. No claim step, no cap, no expiry.

## what that is worth

The desk earns fees on taker volume. Your share works out to roughly **$8 for every $100,000 your referrals trade**. A referral who trades $1M a month earns you about $80 a month; ten of them, $800. It compounds with their activity, not with time.

| your referrals trade | you earn (approx.) |
|---|---|
| $100,000 | $8 |
| $1,000,000 | $80 |
| $10,000,000 | $800 |

The exact figure per trade is the desk's revenue on that trade multiplied by your rate, so maker-heavy referrals earn you less than taker-heavy ones.

## for the referred trader

Entering a code does not change the fees you pay: everyone pays 4.5 / 0. It ties your account to the person who brought you, so they earn on your activity. Codes can only be applied to accounts that have not traded yet, and cannot be changed afterwards. If someone sent you a link, use it before your first trade.

## the referrals page

Under **Referrals** on the desk you can see:

- **Traders Referred**, **Rewards Earned**, **Pending Rewards** (today's accrual, paid at midnight UTC).
- Your code, your link, copy and share buttons. You can **rename** the code until the first trader signs up with it.
- A table of your referrals: address, date joined, total volume, fees paid, your rewards.
- **Rewards History**: a daily chart and a ledger of what was paid and when.

## creators and communities

If you run a community or an audience and want a code before reaching the volume threshold, or a higher rate, message [@thronedefi](https://x.com/thronedefi). Codes issued this way work the same for your audience; only your side changes.

## rules

- One code per account.
- Self-referral, wash trading between your own accounts, or paying people to bind your code with no intent to trade will forfeit rewards and may close the account.
- Rewards are paid in the desk's settlement currency to your desk balance. Withdraw them like any other balance.
