fees

one number to remember: 4.5.

trading fees

fee
taker (market orders, limit orders that cross the spread) 4.5 bps (0.045%)
maker (limit orders that rest and fill) 0 bps

A $10,000 market order pays $4.50. A $10,000 resting limit order that fills pays $0.00.

That is the full price of a trade on the desk. There are no account fees, no deposit fees from the desk, and no withdrawal fee from the desk beyond the network cost of moving funds on-chain.

what the fee does

Fees are the desk's revenue, and the desk's revenue is what pays the court and funds the programs on this site: referral rewards come out of it, season prizes come out of it. So the fee is not just a cost; it is the number that makes everything else here work.

other charges you may see

charge when
funding paid or received periodically while a position is open. See funding.
liquidation fee only if a position is liquidated
network gas when you deposit or withdraw on-chain, paid to the chain, not to the desk

volume tiers

There are none yet. Everyone pays 4.5 / 0. If tiers are introduced they will be announced on @thronedefi and written here first.
