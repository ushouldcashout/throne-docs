points and season zero

every trade earns points. season zero runs 25 september to 2 december 2026.

how points accrue

  • 1 point per $10 of trading volume. A $10,000 position opened and closed earns 1,000 points each way.
  • Points are counted per account, in real time, and shown at throne.network/points and in the desk's Points tab.
  • Points never expire within a season. Each season starts from zero.

beta multiplier

Wallets that were on the beta list before it closed (25 September, 7pm EST) and got into the beta, whether drawn or as a $THRONE holder, earn 2x points on every fill for the whole beta period, until the desk opens to everyone. The multiplier applies to trading points only, not referral bonuses. The beta wallet list is published at throne.network/beta.json so the math can be checked by anyone.

season zero
starts 25 sep 2026, when the desk opens
ends 2 dec 2026
trading competition scored 2 oct to 2 dec 2026. rules and rewards announced on @thronedefi on 26 sep.

what points are for

Points decide your rank on the season leaderboard. The top 10 hold the pieces: king, queen, two rooks, two bishops, two knights, two pawns. Rank decides who gets priority access to the court and who shares the season's prizes. The specific ladder and prizes for season zero are published on @thronedefi and on the points page when the competition is announced, and mirrored here the same day.

anti-wash rules

Points exist to reward real taking. To keep it that way:

  • Per-account caps apply to volume that round-trips within seconds.
  • Obvious wash patterns (matching your own orders across accounts, back-and-forth with a partner at no risk) are reviewed manually and zeroed.
  • Rankings are final only at season end, after review.

what points are not

Points are not a token, not a claim on anything, and carry no promise of future value. They are a leaderboard.
